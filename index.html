What does ShiftScript actually do?

It analyses a meeting transcript with Gemini and produces a structured summary, decisions, follow-ups and proposed tasks. You review the results, approve the work and track it in a shared dashboard.

Do I need to connect Google Meet?

No. You can start by pasting or uploading a transcript, or by adding audio you have permission to process. Google Meet imports are optional.

Does it assign tasks automatically?

It proposes tasks and extracts owners and deadlines where mentioned. You can correct the details before approving them. Missing information needs your input; it should not be treated as a confirmed commitment.

Can my team work together?

Yes. Create shared workspaces, invite members and organise work into projects. Team members see the meetings and tasks available within their workspace permissions.

What happens to my meeting content?

The content you choose to process is sent to Gemini and saved in your selected workspace. Gemini’s handling depends on the API service configuration. For unpaid Gemini processing, use fictional or anonymised test material. Read our Privacy Policy before submitting private content, and only upload audio you have permission to use.
