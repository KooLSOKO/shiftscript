# Client demo: ShiftScript v2

“Your brief was to turn different kinds of meetings into clear, reviewable next steps. ShiftScript captures the transcript, uses Gemini to structure the discussion, and keeps humans in control of task approval.”

1. Show Overview: corporate blue styling, urgency colours and original vector icons.
2. Create **Kopano Portfolio** under Projects. Related meetings/tasks share projects; workspaces separate teams/clients.
3. Start a meeting, select the project and load the fictional sample. Save & close, reopen your private draft.
4. Process the sample. Show summary, decisions, follow-ups, original transcript and quoted proposal evidence. Identify preset sample mode clearly; only describe a live AI request when Gemini is actually used.
5. Select pending proposals. Apply an assignee, deadline and urgency. Approve two; reject one. Nothing enters the tracker until a human approves.
6. Follow a task's link back to its meeting. Add a manual task and checklist; save a completed step, status change and progress update.
7. Filter by project/owner/due date. Export CSV/PDF; show only the filtered work appears in the report. Export a meeting PDF.
8. Invite a prepared teammate account with Member access. Copy/share the link and show the verified-email join flow. Show a Viewer account that reads/exports but cannot edit. Invitations are not automatically emailed.
9. Create another workspace and switch to show isolated data.

Close: “This demonstrates the core transcript-to-action workflow. A later phase can connect Meet, Teams and Zoom. Today's build focuses on reliable review, traceable work and useful collaboration.”
