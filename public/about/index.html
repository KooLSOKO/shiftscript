A clear next step, every time.

ShiftScript by Earny brings meeting records and the work that follows into one shared place. Paste a transcript, transcribe permitted audio, or connect Google to preview notes and transcripts that your Google account can access.

Gemini analyses the selected discussion and proposes a structured summary, decisions, follow-ups, task owners and deadlines where the source supports them. You review and approve the proposed tasks before tracking progress.

Built around your team's work

Meetings with context

Keep tasks connected to the meeting they came from, with agendas and follow-up views.

Shared workspaces

Organise projects, review assignments and track task progress together.

Useful next steps

Prepare Calendar event drafts and send reviewed recaps to the email addresses you choose.

You choose what to import and share

The optional Google connection requests Meet read access and Docs read access to retrieve the source you select. It does not join calls or record Google Meet automatically. Processing sends source text to Gemini and saves it in your selected shared workspace.

Read our AI processing notice before submitting private content. Unpaid Gemini processing is intended here for fictional or anonymised test material. AI output always needs human review.
